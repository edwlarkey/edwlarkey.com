We have been blessed to start a new OCA chapel in Omaha, Nebraska, [St. Tikhon
Chapel](https://sttikhonomaha.com/), under the patronage of St. Tikhon,
Patriarch of Moscow and Enlightener of North America.

Because we are just starting out, we needed a few items so that our Priest
didn't have to pack so many larger items for Divine Liturgy. I built an
analogion (icon stand) and a censer holder. They are pretty simple and plain,
but they are useful for us.

Glory to God for all things!
